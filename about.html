About Us

The story, the people, and the values behind Campus Café.

Our Story

Campus Café began as a class project and a shared love of good coffee. Three students, one espresso machine, and a simple idea: the campus deserved a place where great coffee and great studying could finally share the same table.

Today, we imagine a welcoming corner for students to recharge between lectures, catch up with friends, and make progress on their projects. Whether you are here for a quick cappuccino or a quiet study break, you are always welcome.
